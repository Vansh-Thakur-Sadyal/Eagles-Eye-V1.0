Privacy

Eagles Eye — educational research platform by VisionCoders

What we hold about you

  • Your name, Gmail address, mobile number and organisation, given at sign-up.
  • The record that you accepted the Terms: version, date, time and network address.
  • Sign-in activity: when you signed in, from which address, and whether it succeeded.
  • Your password is not stored here in readable form — it is held as a slow scrypt hash by the authentication service, and one-time codes are stored hashed and expire.

What we do not hold

  • Your video. It is processed on the machine you run the GPU node on; this server receives the agents' conclusions and preview images, not your footage.
  • Payment details. There are none — this platform is not sold.

Who can see it

  • You, and other accounts in your own organisation.
  • The platform administrator, for security and abuse monitoring.
  • Nobody else. Organisations cannot see each other's accounts, cameras or incidents.

Faces and watchlists

  • Bystander faces are blurred by default before any frame leaves the processing machine.
  • Watchlist enrolment requires an approval step and is audited. It must be lawful, necessary, proportionate and time-limited — see the Terms.

Deleting your data

Write to support@visioncoders.online and your account and its personal data will be removed.
