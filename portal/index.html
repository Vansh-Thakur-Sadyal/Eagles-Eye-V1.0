Educational research platform

Every camera you already have,
watched by fifteen agents.

Eagles Eye turns existing CCTV into an attentive second pair of eyes for airports, railway stations, campuses and public venues — crowd density, unattended baggage, restricted-zone entry, and cross-camera person search, each with a plain-language explanation of why it was raised.

Register your site I already have an account

Accounts are approved per organisation — an airport sees only its own cameras and incidents, never another site's.

Crowd intelligence

Density, flow, compression and surge, measured continuously. A density model counts crowds too dense to separate into individual people.

Unattended objects

Bags are linked to the person who carried them, and an escalation is raised when they are left behind — with the owner's last trajectory.

Behaviour

Loitering, counter-flow, running, erratic movement and restricted-zone entry, all thresholded by your own policy rather than ours.

Person of interest

Authorised, audited watchlist matching across every camera in scope, with a plain-language answer of where someone was last seen.

Explainable risk

Each incident carries the arithmetic behind its score. The system recommends; a human decides.

Your own GPU

Video is processed on your machine and only the conclusions reach this server, so footage never has to leave your site.

How it works

  1. Register your site — tell us what kind of site it is: airport, railway station, campus. Your account is bound to that organisation.
  2. Verify — a six-digit code goes to your Gmail address and your phone. Both must be yours.
  3. Attach cameras — webcams, ESP32-CAM boards, RTSP or HTTP streams.
  4. Run the GPU node — one command on a machine with an NVIDIA card; it processes your cameras locally and reports results here.
