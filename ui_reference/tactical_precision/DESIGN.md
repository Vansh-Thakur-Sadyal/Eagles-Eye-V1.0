## Brand & Style
The design system embodies authoritative reliability, quiet situational clarity, and precision-engineered intelligence. Built for mission-critical command environments and public safety operations directors, the interface avoids decorative excess in favor of ultra-high data legibility, low visual cognitive fatigue, and rapid situational awareness.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate / Modern** discipline with **High-Density Technical Functionalism**. The visual posture is vigilant, composed, and uncompromisingly pristine. A dominant light canvas provides high-contrast acuity under daylight or emergency room luminescent lighting, stripping out the dark-mode "sci-fi trope" in favor of crisp government-grade utility, precise micro-lines, and strict semantic signaling.

## Colors
The color architecture establishes an immaculate foundation of pure whites, slate tints, and ultra-crisp structural divides, ensuring tactical semantic states communicate without ambiguity.

### Canvas & Surface Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas (`#f8fafc`):** Neutral slate background that mitigates eye fatigue across expansive high-resolution displays.
- **Card & Component Surfaces (`#ffffff`):** Pure white modules providing high-contrast separation from the surrounding structure.
- **Sub-tier Insets & Headers (`#f1f5f9`):** Light cool-slate fills for table headers, embedded telemetry streams, and secondary toolbars.
- **Structural Dividers (`#e2e8f0` to `#cbd5e1`):** Precise 1px borders enforcing structural clarity across dynamic panels.

### Brand & Intelligence Accents
- **Primary AI Intelligence (`#1d4ed8`):** Deep, crisp cobalt blue indicating algorithmic confidence, autonomous suggestions, active analytical queries, and primary command inputs.
- **Digital Twin & Spatial Geometry (`#7c3aed`):** Electric violet assigned exclusively to geospatial layers, 3D building models, AR camera feeds, and coordinate tracking.
- **Console Slate (`#0f172a`):** Deep naval slate for high-priority typography, metric readouts, and core iconography.

### Semantic Telemetry Tones
- **Operational / Normal (`#059669`):** Emerald green indicating active systems, verified personnel, and stabilized parameters.
- **Elevated / Attention (`#d97706`):** Amber tone signaling triage states, sensor anomalies, and pending authorizations.
- **Critical / Emergency (`#dc2626`):** Piercing crimson reserved strictly for active breaches, dispatched units, severe alerts, and immediate threats.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy balances instant scanning of critical parameters with high-density data presentation. 

- **Geist** serves all structural headers and panel labels, offering geometric precision and tight horizontal kerning that eliminates line bloat in compact card headers.
- **Inter** handles narrative logs, alert descriptions, operator instructions, and interactive form elements, selected for its neutral weight distribution and unmatched micro-legibility.
- **JetBrains Mono** is utilized strictly for non-textual data points: timestamps, coordinates, sensor IDs, AI confidence metrics, and device telemetry. The fixed tabular figures prevent UI jitter during continuous real-time data streaming.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout implements an information-dense, scalable 12-column grid capable of spanning multi-monitor operations decks or condensing into field-ready dispatch terminals. Spacing tokens lean tighter than consumer applications, maximizing screen real estate without causing cognitive crowding.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Operations Console (Desktop & Wall Displays, > 1440px):** 12-column fluid grid with pinned peripheral sidebars (incident queue on the left, telemetry and camera matrix on the right) and a central tactical map/digital twin workspace. `gutter-lg` and `margin-lg` maintain structural clarity across expansive resolutions.
- **Tactical Field Terminal (Tablet, 768px - 1439px):** Peripheral panels fold into expandable bottom sheets and slide-over drawers; grid reduces to 6 columns with `gutter` (0.75rem).
- **Incident Mobile Unit (< 768px):** Single-column stacked layout prioritizing triage notifications, direct action buttons, and live map viewports, leveraging `margin` (1rem).

## Elevation & Depth
This design system avoids theatrical drop shadows in favor of a crisp architectural hierarchy built on **low-contrast outlines** paired with **micro-ambient diffusion**.

1. **Flat / Base Layer (`0dp`):** Slate background surfaces (`#f8fafc`) housing non-interactive structural divisions.
2. **Surface Layer (`1dp`):** White card modules (`#ffffff`) defined by a hairline 1px border (`#e2e8f0`). Elevated slightly by an ambient diffusion of `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
3. **Active Focus / Flyout Layer (`2dp`):** Contextual filter menus, map tooltips, and flyout operational panels. Defined by `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` over a 1px border (`#cbd5e1`).
4. **Overlay / Critical Incident Modal (`3dp`):** Emergency override dialogs, priority dispatches, and full video feed expansions. Backed by a 40% cool-slate backdrop wash with `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes
To reinforce an atmosphere of military-grade discipline and structural efficiency, the roundedness scale is locked to **Soft (Level 1)**. 

- Standard inputs, buttons, card enclosures, and metric containers feature a compact `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- Structural modal dialogs and outer master panels use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Status indicators, tactical telemetry badges, and live connection pings deliberately break this geometry via full pill shapes (`9999px`) to immediately separate metadata tags from actionable rectangular card modules.

## Components

### Buttons & Trigger Actions
- **Primary Operational:** High-density cobalt background (`#1d4ed8`), crisp white text, 4px corner radius, standard 32px height for high density (`px-3 py-1.5`). Hover transitions to `#1e40af` without size shifts.
- **Secondary Neutral:** Pure white background, hairline slate border (`#e2e8f0`), dark slate text (`#0f172a`). Hover introduces background tint (`#f8fafc`) and border tint (`#cbd5e1`).
- **Emergency Action / Critical Override:** Crimson background (`#dc2626`), high-contrast white text, subtle pulse ring animation on critical state triggers.

### Tactical Badges & Chips
- Designed with high horizontal density, featuring a continuous pill radius (`rounded-full`) and fixed height (20px to 24px).
- **Structure:** 6px solid circular telemetry pip on the leading edge, accompanied by uppercase mono label text (`label-sm`).
- **Variants:**
  - *Operational:* Green tint background (`#ecfdf5`), border `#a7f3d0`, pip `#059669`, text `#065f46`.
  - *Attention:* Amber tint background (`#fffbeb`), border `#fde68a`, pip `#d97706`, text `#92400e`.
  - *Critical:* Crimson tint background (`#fef2f2`), border `#fecaca`, pip `#dc2626`, text `#991b1b`.
  - *AI Automated:* Blue tint background (`#eff6ff`), border `#bfdbfe`, pip `#1d4ed8`, text `#1e40af`.
  - *Digital Twin / AR:* Purple tint background (`#faf5ff`), border `#e9d5ff`, pip `#7c3aed`, text `#6b21a8`.

### Incident Cards & Data Containers
- Crisp white surfaces encased in a 1px border (`#e2e8f0`).
- Card headers utilize a standardized 36px bar with sub-surface fill (`#f8fafc`), bottom separator (`#e2e8f0`), and uppercase Geist label.
- Metrics are anchored by large Geist numeric readouts and companion JetBrains Mono delta indicators.

### Form Inputs & Filters
- **Text & Numeric Inputs:** Pure white fill, inset 1px border (`#cbd5e1`), height standard 32px (`text-xs`), placeholder tone `#94a3b8`. Focus state introduces a crisp 1px ring in primary cobalt (`#1d4ed8`) without outer glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Scaled down to 14px x 14px with 2px corner radius on checkboxes. Checked state applies `#1d4ed8` with sharp white tick marks.

### Telemetry Tables & Incident Feeds
- Compact row height (32px to 36px) enabling 20+ rows in immediate view without pagination.
- Alternating row interaction: standard white, hovering applies `#f8fafc` instant cross-row highlight. Cell text relies on `body-sm` for incident descriptions and `data-mono-sm` for system identifiers and timestamps.

### Specialized AI & Command Components
- **AI Recommendation Banner:** Clean slate border accented by a 3px vertical cobalt edge on the left margin, signaling an algorithmic insight without interrupting manual dispatch control.
- **Spatial / Digital Twin Viewport Frame:** Enclosed camera feed and 3D mesh container bordered in `#e2e8f0` with absolute-positioned violet corner brackets, conveying real-time coordinate mapping.
